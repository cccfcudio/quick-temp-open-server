Multi Profiles Camera — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Multi Profiles Camera application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

The App is a camera utility. Its core features run entirely on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your media.

  • Photos and videos — captured media is written to the storage location you select (a folder on your device, or a folder you pick through the system file picker). We never upload your media to our servers.
  • Location — only if you explicitly enable GPS tagging. The location is written into the photo's EXIF metadata and stays on your device.
  • Microphone — used for video audio and voice notes attached to media. Audio is stored only in files on your device.
  • Profile settings — your camera profiles (name, resolution, watermark text, storage folder, etc.) are stored locally in the App's private storage. They are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or any account information, because the App has no account system.

3. Advertising and Device Identifiers

The free version of the App displays advertising through Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier) and app set ID;
  • device information (model, operating system version, language, approximate region);
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and IP address.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's privacy policy. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we present a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before any personalised advertising is served, and you may decline personalised ads.

Purchasing the Pro version removes advertising; the advertising SDK then does not serve ads.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. See Google Ads policies.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — consent management. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see or store your card information.

5. Permissions and Why We Need Them

  • Camera — required to take photos and record video.
  • Microphone — required to record audio with videos and voice notes.
  • Location — optional; only used to write GPS coordinates into photo EXIF metadata. Declining it does not prevent you from using the camera.
  • Notifications — optional; used to show capture status.
  • Network access — required to load ads and to validate in-app purchases.

The App does not request broad file access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) and does not request permission to read your existing photo library. Access to a custom storage folder is granted only through the Android system file picker, for the single folder you choose.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your media files remain in the storage location you chose and are under your control; delete them with any file manager or from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored profile settings. Because we do not hold any data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Data Security

Your media and notes are stored locally on your device. Please keep your device locked and up to date to reduce the risk of unauthorised access.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced through the App or the store listing, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at:
ccc.fcudio@gmail.com

Multi Profiles Camera 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月

一、適用範圍

本隱私權政策適用於 Multi Profiles Camera 應用程式（以下簡稱「本應用」）。使用本應用即表示您同意本政策。

二、我們收集與處理的資料

本應用為相機工具，核心功能完全在您的裝置上執行，我們沒有接收您媒體檔案的伺服器。

  • 相片與影片：拍攝的媒體儲存於您指定的位置（裝置上的資料夾，或您透過系統檔案選擇器挑選的資料夾）。我們不會將您的媒體上傳至我們的伺服器。
  • 位置資訊：僅在您主動啟用 GPS 標記時使用。座標會寫入相片的 EXIF metadata，只留在您的裝置。
  • 麥克風：用於錄影收音與媒體的語音註解，音訊只存在您裝置的檔案中。
  • 設定檔資料：您的相機設定檔（名稱、解析度、水印文字、儲存資料夾等）儲存於本應用的私有空間，不會傳送給我們。

本應用沒有帳號系統，因此我們不會收集您的姓名、電郵、聯絡人或任何帳號資料。

三、廣告與裝置識別碼

本應用免費版透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。為投放廣告，Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能收集及處理：

  • 您裝置的廣告 ID（可重設的識別碼）及應用程式集 ID；
  • 裝置資訊（型號、作業系統版本、語言、大約地區）；
  • 廣告互動資料（曝光、點擊）及 IP 位址。

上述資料由 Google 收集，並非由我們收集，受 Google 的隱私權政策規範。在歐洲經濟區、英國及瑞士，我們會在投放個人化廣告前顯示同意對話框（Google User Messaging Platform），您可拒絕個人化廣告。

購買專業版後廣告會被移除，廣告 SDK 亦不會再投放廣告。

四、第三方服務

  • Google AdMob：廣告投放。
  • Google User Messaging Platform：同意管理。
  • Google Play Billing：應用程式內購買。付款資料由 Google 處理，我們不會看到或儲存您的信用卡資訊。

五、權限說明

  • 相機：拍照與錄影的必要權限。
  • 麥克風：錄影收音與語音註解的必要權限。
  • 位置：可選，只用於把 GPS 座標寫入相片 EXIF。拒絕亦不影響拍照。
  • 通知：可選，用於顯示拍攝狀態。
  • 網路：載入廣告及驗證應用程式內購買。

本應用不會請求廣泛檔案存取權（MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE），亦不會請求讀取您現有相簿的權限。自訂儲存資料夾只透過 Android 系統檔案選擇器授權，且僅限您親自挑選的那一個資料夾。

六、資料保留與刪除

您的媒體檔案保存在您選擇的儲存位置，由您自行控制；您可以用任何檔案管理員或在本應用內刪除。解除安裝本應用會一併移除其本機設定檔資料。由於我們不持有您的任何資料，我們這邊沒有任何資料需要刪除。

七、資料安全

您的媒體與註解儲存在您的裝置本機。請設定螢幕鎖定並保持系統更新，以降低未授權存取的風險。

八、兒童隱私

本應用並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

九、政策變更

我們可能不定期更新本隱私權政策。重大變更將透過應用程式或商店說明公告，並更新上方「最後更新」日期。
